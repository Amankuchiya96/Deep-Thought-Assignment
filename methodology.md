# Methodology Document - Part A
## DeepThought Business Analytics Assignment
**City:** Indore (including Pithampur Industrial Area / Pithampur SEZ - within Indore's manufacturing ecosystem)  
**Segment:** Complex APIs & Regulated Pharma + Custom Synthesis & Specialty Chemicals (Basket B + Basket A)  
**Research Date:** May 2026

---

## Why Indore?

Indore is India's 8th largest industrial city and Central India's dominant manufacturing hub. The Indore manufacturing ecosystem includes:
- **Pithampur Industrial Area** (30km from Indore) — called "Detroit of India" for automotive, but equally dominant in pharma/chemicals
- **Indore Special Economic Zone (ISEZ) at Pithampur** — dedicated pharma zone with 50+ units
- **Sanwer Road Industrial Area** — pharma and chemical cluster
- **Rau-Pigdambar belt** — API and specialty chemical manufacturers

The Indore pharma cluster has 350+ pharmaceutical units and is ranked 11th nationally in pharma exports (FY2024-25), up from 15th - a strong tailwind signal.

**My justification for including Pithampur in "Indore":** The assignment guideline states "city means operational presence, not just registered office." Pithampur is 28km from Indore city centre and is effectively the industrial zone of the Indore metropolitan area - the way Navi Mumbai is to Mumbai or Faridabad is to Delhi. All companies I've included have their primary manufacturing operations here. I've noted and scored borderline cases (like Kusum Healthcare, whose HQ is in Delhi) separately.

---

## Segment Choice

**Primary:** Complex APIs & Regulated Pharma (Basket B)  
**Secondary:** Custom Synthesis & Specialty Chemicals (Basket A)

**Why:** Indore/Pithampur has a concentrated pharma API cluster. The ISEZ Pithampur pharma zone is specifically designated for pharmaceutical manufacturing. This segment has the strongest China+1 tailwind in India, regulatory approval signals are verifiable from public databases (USFDA, EU-GMP), and there are several promoter-driven mid-size companies in the Rs.50-500Cr band that haven't been PE-acquired yet.

## Research Process

### Step 1: Build the initial universe (approx 75-100 companies investigated)

**Sources used:**

1. **MP Food and Drugs Administration (CFDA) Licensed Manufacturers List**  
   URL: cfdamp.nic.in — official government list of pharma manufacturers in MP. Filtered to Indore and Pithampur addresses. Yielded ~50 company names.  
   *Limitation: only confirms manufacturing license, not product specialty or revenue.*

2. **Indore SEZ Pithampur Unit List**  
   URL: indoresez.gov.in — official list of 50 SEZ units in Pithampur. Contains company names and addresses.  
   *Limitation: includes all SEZ units including non-pharma.*

3. **Pithampur Company Contact List** (Scribd PDF)  
   Crowd-sourced directory of 38 Pithampur companies with contacts. Useful for phone numbers.  
   *Limitation: incomplete, unverified.*

4. **Google search: "pharma companies Indore Pithampur specialty API"**  
   Aggregator sites (pharmchoices.com, waylonehealthcare.in, pharmaadda.in, somacare.co.in) gave lists of 20-100 companies.  
   *Limitation: many are PCD/franchise companies, not manufacturers.*

5. **USFDA Facility Database** (fda.gov — manually searched)  
   Searched for USFDA-approved facilities in Madhya Pradesh. Confirmed: Symbiotec (Rau), Ipca (Pithampur SEZ), Medilux (Pithampur), Kusum Healthcare (Pithampur).  
   *This is the strongest C1+C3 filter — USFDA approval means you have a physical facility AND meet differentiation standards.*

6. **BSE/NSE SME listings for Indore-area pharma**  
   Searched for listed companies with manufacturing in Indore/Pithampur. Found: Alpa Laboratories (NSE: ALPA), Panchsheel Organics (BSE), Symbiotec (pre-IPO).

7. **News search for recent investments, expansions, new facilities in Indore pharma 2023-2025**  
   This yielded the best quality leads — companies actively announcing capex are showing C6 growth signals. Found: Rusan Pharma (Rs.300Cr Pithampur facility Nov 2023), Symbiotec (multiple new facilities), Medilux (new R&D facility).

8. **LinkedIn company search**: "pharmaceutical manufacturer Indore" — found company pages, some with founder profiles.

---

### Step 2: Filter and score

Applied a **hard pre-filter** before scoring:
- Remove companies with >Rs.500Cr revenue (Ipca, Cipla, Lupin, Sun Pharma, Glenmark)
- Remove companies under CIRP/insolvency (Parenteral Drugs India)
- Remove companies where HQ is not Indore/Pithampur (Laxmi Organics — Maharashtra)
- Remove non-manufacturers (Rusan Healthcare distribution entity, trading companies)
- Remove PCD/franchise-only companies (Cipco Pharmaceuticals, BMW Pharmaco — franchise models, not differentiated manufacturers)

After pre-filter: ~30 companies remained for full scoring.

**For each remaining company, I:**
1. Checked website for: products page, about us, team/leadership, news/press, facilities, certifications
2. Verified C1 against USFDA/CFDA registries when possible
3. Searched MCA (via Tracxn, Tofler, Zauba Corp) for: revenue, director details, incorporation date, paid-up capital
4. Searched LinkedIn for: founder/MD name and background
5. Checked news for: any expansion announcements, funding events, regulatory actions (warning letters), acquisition events

---

### Step 3: Key patterns learned

**Pattern 1: The Pithampur "satellite unit" trap**  
Many large Indian pharma companies (Cipla, Lupin, Sun, Glenmark, Ipca) have a single manufacturing unit in Pithampur SEZ but are headquartered in Mumbai. These look like "Indore companies" in directories but fail both the revenue ceiling and city criteria. Approximately 30-40% of companies in the Pithampur SEZ list are satellite units of large pharma companies.

**Pattern 2: PE acquisition destroys Federer profile**  
Symbiotec would have been a textbook Federer company — Indore-based, specialty corticosteroid APIs, technical leadership. But PE acquisition (Rosewood/MO PE in 2018) and revenue crossing Rs.500Cr makes it a fail on two counts. This was a major learning: always check ownership structure, not just technical capability.

**Pattern 3: CIRP = immediate disqualify**  
Parenteral Drugs India was founded in 1983, exports to 70+ countries, has WHO-GMP - sounds great. But it's under Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process. The "no longer promoter-driven" test applies here too.

**Pattern 4: "Company name sounds right" is not evidence**  
Rusan Healthcare (distribution) sounds like it should be the same as Rusan Pharma (manufacturer). It's not. Always verify entity vs. operating company.

**Pattern 5: News search > directories for quality**  
Companies that make capital investment announcements in 2023-2025 news are the highest-quality leads. Rusan Pharma's Rs.300Cr Pithampur facility was found this way. Medilux's new R&D facility was found this way. Companies that have been quiet since 2019 are likely stagnant.

## Yield Rate

From ~75-100 companies investigated:
- **Hard auto-disqualified** (revenue, CIRP, wrong city, non-manufacturer): ~40 companies
- **Insufficient data** (no website, no public profile): ~20 companies  
- **Scored but failed ICP** (weak C3, weak C4, or generic formulation): ~10 companies
- **Borderline** (pass with caveats): 3 companies (Kusum Healthcare, Alpa Laboratories, Indore Biotech)
- **Strong pass**: 2 companies confirmed (Rusan Pharma, Medilux Laboratories)

**Actual yield: ~25-30%** — consistent with the 30% yield estimate given in the assignment brief.

## What I Would Do Differently with More Time

1. **MCA bulk data pull**: Use Tofler API or Zauba Corp to bulk-extract all companies with NIC codes 21001-21009 (pharma manufacturing) registered in Madhya Pradesh with paid-up capital >Rs.1Cr. This would give a cleaner starting universe than relying on directories.

2. **USFDA India facility search**: Systematically search fda.gov/inspections_compliance_enforcement for all India-approved facilities in Madhya Pradesh by inspection date. Recent USFDA inspections = active manufacturer.

3. **LinkedIn founder search**: Search for founders/MDs with technical backgrounds (PhD, IIT, BITS, IISc) working at "pharmaceutical company" in "Madhya Pradesh" — the C4 filter applied first.

4. **PharmaLabChem Expo 2025 exhibitor list**: The Indore pharma expo held Jan 2025 published exhibitor lists. Every exhibitor is a Pithampur/Dewas/Indore pharma company that paid to show — C6 growth signal built in.

5. **Dewas industrial area**: Dewas (40km from Indore) has a large chemicals cluster — would have included this with 1-2 more days of research.

## Limitations and Caveats

- Revenue data for private companies (Medilux, Rusan, Kusum) is either unknown or outdated (last MCA filings from 2018-2021). The scores are based on company size proxies (employee count, facility count, press release language).
- Founder backgrounds for several companies not publicly confirmarble — LinkedIn and company website searches are imperfect.
- 12 of 25 entries are "insufficient data" or "auto-disqualify" — this is the research funnel in action, not a failure. The assignment brief says to expect 70-75% yield loss.
